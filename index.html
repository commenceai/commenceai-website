What we do

Four services, one goal: technology your people actually use

Each engagement is scoped to your size, budget and pace, and ends with your team able to carry on without us.

AI adoption

We find the places where AI genuinely saves time or improves decisions in your business, then help you pilot, govern and scale it safely, in line with UK GDPR and the EU AI Act.

Outcomes for you

  • A prioritised list of AI use cases with clear business value
  • Working pilots on your own data and tools
  • A simple AI policy and governance model your team can follow
  • Staff trained to use AI tools confidently and responsibly

Enterprise Architecture training

Practical EA training that helps your people connect strategy, processes, data and systems, so technology investments line up with where the business is going.

Outcomes for you

  • Teams who can map capabilities, processes and systems
  • A lightweight EA approach that fits an SME, not a bank
  • Better, faster decisions on technology spend
  • A current-state and target-state view of your business

CRM adoption

Many CRMs are bought but barely used. We set yours up around how your teams really sell and serve, clean up the data, and coach people until using it becomes a habit.

Outcomes for you

  • A CRM configured around your actual sales and service process
  • Clean, trusted customer data in one place
  • Dashboards leaders use to run the business
  • Higher day-to-day use across your teams

SAP to Salesforce migration

A carefully planned move of customer, sales and service processes from SAP to Salesforce: data mapping, integration, testing and cutover, with minimal disruption to day-to-day trading.

Outcomes for you

  • A clear migration roadmap, budget and risk plan
  • Accurate, validated data in Salesforce
  • Integrations that keep finance and operations in step
  • A smooth go-live with trained, supported users

How we work

A clear path from idea to everyday use

Every engagement follows four stages. You can start with a short assessment and decide on next steps from there.

  1. 01

    Assess

    We learn how your business runs today: goals, processes, systems, data and skills. You get an honest view of where you stand.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We agree priorities, scope, budget and success measures, and turn them into a practical roadmap with quick wins first.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    We build, configure, migrate or train in short, visible stages, with regular check-ins so there are no surprises.

  4. 04

    Adopt

    We stay with you through go-live, coaching and handover, so the change sticks and your team owns it.
